# Input and Output Boards for RCA/XLR
We offer several PCB solutions for routing audio signals. Our PCBs are designed to be used with our control boards and amplifiers. We have build our PCBs to minimize the wire length for small signals and maximize the signal to noise ratio; just like with our amplifiers, we have integrated a RFI/EMI shield directly into the PCB. Our PCBs come with through-hole connections for easy assembly and maintenance.

## Features
- Both signal and ground are switched on RCA boards to prevent ground loops between digital devices
- Minimize wire length for small signals
- Maximize signal to noise ratio
- RFI/EMI shield integrated into the PCB
- Through-hole connections for easy assembly and maintenance

## PCB Specifications
- Thickness: 2.0mm
- Material: FR4 S1000H TG150
- Layers: 4
- Finish: Immersion Gold (ENIG)
- Copper weight: 1oz
- Solder mask: Matte black
- Silkscreen: White
